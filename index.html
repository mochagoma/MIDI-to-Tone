      About MIDI to Tone
    


    

      This converter takes advantage of how most click-wheel (classic and nano) iPods have a piezoelectric buzzer—a small electronic component that turns electricity into sound—which is used to produce the iPod’s signature click-wheel sound.

      The buzzer can also be used to play sequences of buzzes at different frequencies and pitches, such as when an alarm goes off. Here’s where this converter comes into play: it can convert any ordinary MIDI file into a special .tone file compatible with most Apple iPods, which can then be played when an alarm goes off.

      To add a .tone file to your iPod, drag the file into the iPod_Control/Tones folder on your iPod’s filesystem.

      The iPod_Control folder is hidden by default on Mac computers. To show it (and other hidden files and folders), use the keyboard shortcut ⌘ + Shift + period.
